This policy covers the WardAxis app for Mac, iPhone, iPad and Apple TV, and the extensions and helper that come with it. Agnidia ("we") publishes WardAxis.

## Summary

WardAxis checks network traffic, web pages and mail on your device. What it checks stays on your device. We do not collect personal data, we do not track you, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

## What WardAxis checks, and where

Each protection works only after you turn it on and approve it in system settings. You can turn each one off at any time.

| Protection | What it checks | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Network filter, transparent proxy, DNS protection (Mac) | The network connections and domain names that apps open | On your Mac |
| Web address filter (Mac, iPhone, iPad) | The web addresses that your device opens | On your device |
| Network protection (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV) | Network traffic, through a local VPN configuration | On your device; the traffic does not go to our servers |
| Safari extension (Mac, iPhone, iPad) | The text that you send on the AI and policy sites that you register, and no other site | On your device |
| Mail extension (Mac) | The recipients, text and attachments of messages before you send them | On your Mac |
| Background helper (Mac) | Nothing by itself; it keeps your protections and policy up to date while the app is closed | On your Mac |

The text that a check matches is never stored, logged or sent.

## What we collect

Nothing. WardAxis sends no data about you or your device to us or to third parties. It has no advertising, no analytics SDK and no third-party tracking.

## Data on your device

- **Settings and history.** Your settings and a history of decisions (for example, "a connection was blocked") stay in the app's storage on your device.
- **Diagnostic log.** WardAxis keeps a log on your device for troubleshooting. It has no message text, page content or credentials. It leaves your device only if you share it from Settings, and then only with the recipient you choose.
- **Credentials.** If your organization manages your device, the credential that identifies the device is stored in the device Keychain. It does not leave the device in a backup or iCloud Keychain.

## Optional usage statistics

The app can ask whether you want to share which screens and buttons you use. This is off unless you turn it on. These statistics never contain web addresses, host names, file names, message text or anything that WardAxis checks. No version of WardAxis sends them yet. We will update this policy before one does.

## Devices that an organization manages

Your employer or school can connect WardAxis to its own management server, so that the device gets the organization's policy. That organization operates the server, not us. The server learns that the device is enrolled and which policy it uses. It does not receive the content that WardAxis checks. The organization's own privacy notice covers its server.

## Retention and deletion

All WardAxis data is on your device. Delete the app to delete it. On a Mac, also remove the extensions and the helper as the app describes. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete.

## Children

WardAxis is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If WardAxis starts to collect any data, we will update this policy, the App Store privacy label and the app's consent screens before that version is released, and we will ask for your consent in the app.
